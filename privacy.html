2. How We Use Your Data

We process collected data solely to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the diagnostic platform
  • Authenticate accounts and enforce rate limits
  • Display community threads and reputation scores
  • Prevent spam, abuse, and Terms violations

3. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use essential browser storage and standard analytics:

  • Essential Storage — Authentication tokens, language selection, and bookmarked fault codes.
  • Analytics — Google Analytics and Yandex Metrika collect aggregated anonymous usage telemetry.

Advertising Partners (Google AdSense)

We use Google AdSense to fund free access to the diagnostic database. Google and its partners may use advertising cookies.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits.
  • You may manage or opt out of personalized ads at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

4. Data Sharing

We never sell or rent personal data. Data is shared only for:

  • Legal compliance — valid court or regulatory orders
  • Edge Infrastructure — Cloudflare network delivery and DDoS protection
  • Public Forum Contributions — threads and replies published by you

5. Data Security

Security controls include:

  • Cryptographic password hashing (never stored in plain text)
  • TLS encryption across all client-server traffic
  • Signed, expiring session tokens
Always use a strong, unique password for your account.

6. Your Rights

You retain full control over your account data:

  • Access & Correction — Update profile details directly in your account settings.
  • Account Deletion — Request account removal via our support desk.

Contact codesbimmer@gmail.com for privacy requests.

7. Data Retention

Account credentials are retained while your account remains active. Public technical forum threads remain part of the community archive unless removed.

8. Age Restriction

BimmerCodes is intended for users aged 16 and older.

9. Changes to This Policy

Material updates will be reflected in the revision date at the top of this page.

10. Contact
