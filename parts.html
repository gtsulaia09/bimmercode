Parts Verification Protocol

How specialists verify BMW replacement parts.

BMW frequently revises part numbers mid-cycle (LCI updates, TU engine revisions, SULEV N51/N26 variants). Always cross-check the 11-digit ETK part number against your vehicle's build date.

01 · Genuine BMW 11-Digit ETK

Factory BMW boxed components required or strongly advised for piezoelectric/solenoid injectors (Index 12), NOx/Lambda sensors, IBS cables, and coded control modules.

02 · Original Equipment (OE) Tier-1 Supplier

Manufactured on the same line as Genuine BMW parts without the roundel stamp — e.g., Pierburg (water pumps/solenoids), Bosch (coils/MAF/HDP), BorgWarner, Mahle/Behr, ZF, and Lemförder.

03 · Hardware & Seals One-Time-Use

Aluminum torque-to-yield bolts (water pump, VANOS gear, oil pan), Viton O-rings, and PTFE injector decoupling elements must not be reused during repair.
