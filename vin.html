WBA · WBS · WBY Germany

WBA identifies BMW AG passenger vehicles, WBS designates BMW M GmbH motorsport models, and WBY marks BMW i electric/hybrid architecture.

5UX · 5UM · 4US Spartanburg, USA

5UX and 5UM identify BMW X-Series SAVs (X3, X4, X5, X6, X7, XM) manufactured at Plant Spartanburg, South Carolina.

Where to Find VIN Chassis Locations
