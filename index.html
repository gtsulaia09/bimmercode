Technical Reference

Frequently asked diagnostic questions.

What is the difference between BMW hex codes and OBD-II P-codes?
Generic OBD-II scanners report standardized 5-character emissions codes (such as P0171 or P0128). BMW factory diagnostic systems (ISTA, INPA) read 4-digit or 6-digit manufacturer hex codes (such as 2A82, 118001, or A0B4) directly from each control module, providing far more specific component and circuit fault detail. BimmerCodes indexes both formats.
How do I read fault codes from all BMW modules?
Connect a K+DCAN cable (E-Series), ENET cable (F/G-Series), or compatible Bluetooth OBD adapter to the 16-pin diagnostic socket in the driver footwell. Use BMW ISTA, INPA, BimmerLink, MHD, or a multi-system scanner to poll DME/DDE, EGS, DSC, CAS/FEM/BDC, and SRS modules, then look up each stored code here.
Which BMW models and engines are supported?
Our database covers E-Series (E36, E39, E46, E60, E70, E90), F-Series (F10, F20, F25, F30, F80), and G-Series (G01, G05, G20, G30, G80) vehicles across M54, N20, N47, N52, N54, N55, N63, B47, B48, B58, S55, and S58 powertrains.
